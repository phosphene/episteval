# `episteval` Reference Wiki

The `episteval` reference wiki documents the philosophical, mathematical, and methodological foundations of automated epistemic evaluation, hypothetico-deductive verification, and inferential warrant.

---

## Index of Articles

### 1. Methodological Foundations
- **[Braithwaite's Hypothetico-Deductive Apparatus](foundations/braithwaite-hypothetico-deductive.md):**
  R.B. Braithwaite's *Scientific Explanation* (1953) as the formal model for scientific theories as deductive systems, the hierarchy of hypotheses, and empirical establishment vs. probability.
- **[Wisdom's Testability Framework](foundations/wisdom-testability-framework.md):**
  J.O. Wisdom's *Foundations of Inference in Natural Science* (1952), the distinction between empirical generalizations and non-instantial hypotheses, and testability-by-deduction across scientific domains.

### 2. Evaluative Concepts & Architecture
- **[The Epistemic Matrix & Lexicographic Gating](concepts/lexicographic-gating.md):**
  The 4-level ($L_1$ to $L_4$) evaluation matrix, non-compensatory lexicographic gating, and the mathematical mechanics of certified warrant tiers ($T_1$, $T_2$, $T_3$).
- **[The Dual-Faceted Object Model](concepts/dual-faceted-object-model.md):**
  Reconciling downstream continuous scalar consumption ($[0.0, 1.0]$ via Python's numeric data model) with formal structural provenance (derivation hypergraph and AST).
